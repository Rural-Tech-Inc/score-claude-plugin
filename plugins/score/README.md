# S.C.O.R.E. for Claude

S.C.O.R.E. is a point of sale and inventory system for small businesses: shops, restaurants, bakeries and services. This plugin connects Claude to your S.C.O.R.E. account, so you can ask about your business in plain words and Claude answers from your own stores.

## Sign in

The first time you use it, Claude asks you to sign in. A S.C.O.R.E. page opens in your browser; sign in with your usual account and press **Approve**. No password or key is ever typed into Claude.

Claude works as you, with your role and permissions in each store. It can never do more than you can in the app.

## What you can ask

- "Which stores do I have?"
- "What did I sell today in the bakery?"
- "Which products are low on stock?"
- "Create a store called Café Sol in Ecuador, a restaurant."

Claude can read anything you can read in the app and create stores (your plan's store limit applies). Changing products and recording sales come in a later version. Deleting a store, your subscription and card payments stay in the S.C.O.R.E. app.

## Ending Claude's access

In S.C.O.R.E. on the web, open **Connections**, then **Claude**, then **Disconnect**. Your other sessions stay signed in.
